Daily Briefing

Daily Briefing is a personal tool used only by its owner, Chang Hsiu Chi. Every morning it reads the owner's own Gmail inbox, sorts incoming mail with Gmail labels, and sends the owner a short summary of important emails and upcoming reservations via LINE.

Daily Briefing 是張修齊個人使用的工具：每天早上讀取本人的 Gmail，自動分類信件，並透過 LINE 傳送重要信件與近期預約的摘要給本人。

This app is not offered to the public and has no other users.
