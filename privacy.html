Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-12

Who uses this app

Daily Briefing is a personal tool. Its only user is its owner, Chang Hsiu Chi, who authorizes access to their own Gmail accounts.

What data is accessed

With the owner's permission, the app reads email messages and labels in the owner's Gmail accounts, creates Gmail labels and filters, archives messages, and sends messages from the owner's account to the owner.

How the data is used

Email content is used only to sort the owner's mail and to produce a daily summary that is sent to the owner via LINE. Email content may be processed by an AI model (Claude by Anthropic) under the owner's own account solely to create that summary.

Sharing and storage

No data is sold or shared with anyone else. The app does not keep copies of email content; it stores only the time of the last run and a list of addresses the owner has emailed before, in the owner's private storage.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Daily Briefing's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
